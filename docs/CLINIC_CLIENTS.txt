Clinic clients without a Vetly account
======================================

Workflow
--------
1. In the vet's Clients page, add a guardian and their pets. Email is optional.
2. Open the guardian card and select New appointment. The guardian is already
   selected; pets are available even without a Vetly login.
3. Use the normal examination and patient-history flows to record clinical care.
4. To connect an account, add the guardian's email and send an invitation.
5. A new guardian registers through the invitation and verifies their email.
   An existing guardian uses the existing-account option on the invitation page,
   signs in and accepts it with the same verified email.

Linking preserves the same pet, appointment and medical-history identities.
Existing medication records stay attached to those pets. Reminder and notification
ownership moves in the same transaction. Private clinic/contact/pet notes remain
private to the clinic. An ordinary signup or a matching name/email alone does not
claim a clinical card; the verified invitation flow is required.

Clinic-only identities have no login email or password. Invitation links expire
and are single-use. Another account cannot accept them. Vets retain access to
their patients, not all unrelated pets belonging to a linked guardian.
Removing a clinic pet hides it without deleting its clinical history. A client
card containing pets or appointment history cannot be permanently removed.
Unknown breed, age, weight and gender remain unspecified until entered.

Release / migration
-------------------
Deploy backend and frontend together. Back up the database and validate restoration
before running Alembic upgrade head. Revision d9e031a7b423 follows c8d920f6a312,
creates clinical identities for existing clinic pets and keeps private notes in
their original records. A destructive downgrade is intentionally unavailable;
rollback requires the verified backup and the matching application version.

On 2026-11-07 the migration was applied to the local development database only.
A restored copy was migrated separately, preserving existing pet, appointment,
medical-event, medication, reminder and client IDs and private pet notes.
Production has not been migrated or deployed by this change.

Validation
----------
118 backend tests passed, including no-account booking/history, verified new and
existing-account claims, access boundaries, expired/replayed/wrong-email invites,
soft deletion, and concurrent booking during account linking.
Frontend production build and lint passed (existing lint warnings remain).
Browser smoke verified the client-card booking action, preselected guardian and
missing-pet guidance. End-to-end record mutations were tested in an isolated
PostgreSQL database; local UI verification did not send invitations.
